## Selected projects

- **Dexterous cable insertion** — Intrinsic AI for Industry Challenge,
  2026. End-to-end LeRobot pipeline (Gazebo, ROS 2) for contact-rich
  cable insertion; 1K+ tier-filtered episodes via DART corrective
  rollouts; ~50% full-insertion rate in sim.
- **Bilateral teleoperation on SO-101** — dual-arm LeRobot extension
  with haptic contact feedback, motion assist, and gravity hold on
  low-cost servos.
  [github.com/YosubShin/lerobot/tree/bilateral-teleop](https://github.com/YosubShin/lerobot/tree/bilateral-teleop)
- **SO-101 field notes** — the two-part series on this site: [training a
  diffusion policy on a $300 arm]({% post_url 2026-08-19-so101-part1-training %})
  and [measuring the evaluation itself]({% post_url 2026-08-19-so101-part2-evaluation %}).
- **koa-cli** — open-source multi-cluster Slurm submission tool.
  [github.com/YosubShin/koa](https://github.com/YosubShin/koa)
